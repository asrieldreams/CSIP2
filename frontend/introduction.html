How It Works

From report to protection in minutes

Our community-driven pipeline turns individual reports into real-time protection for everyone on the platform.

01

You report a scam

Submit a scam encounter — SMS, call, email, or link — directly via our web portal or mobile app in under 60 seconds.

02

AI + community validation

Reports are cross-referenced with existing data and verified by our automated AI pipeline and trusted community moderators.

03

Database updated instantly

Verified threats are pushed to our central scam database — URLs, numbers, patterns — accessible across all platform features.

04

Everyone is protected

The scanner app, Telegram bot, and browser extension immediately warn other users — turning one report into community-wide protection.
